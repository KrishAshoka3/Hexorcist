# Product Context: HEXORCIST — Universal Automotive CAN-Bus & FinTech Escrow Chimera

## Core Architecture
HEXORCIST bridges low-level hexadecimal automotive hardware protocols (SAE J1979 OBD-II Modes 01/03/04/06 and ISO 14229 UDS Modes 22/2F/31) with an AI Diagnostic Necromancer (powered by Google Cloud Vertex AI) and an autonomous FinTech Repair Sinking-Fund Ledger.

## Universal Vehicle Support
All telemetry flows through the table-driven `UNIVERSAL_PID_REGISTRY` supporting four powertrain classes:
1. `GERMAN_V6_DSG`: Euro 3.0L Supercharged V6 + 7-Speed DSG (0B5)
2. `JDM_TWIN_ROTARY`: JDM 1.3L Twin-Turbo Rotary (13B-REW)
3. `AWD_800V_EV`: 800V Dual-Motor SiC Inverter EV Sedan
4. `HEAVY_DIESEL_TOW`: 6.7L High-Output Turbo-Diesel Tow Rig

## Financial & Accounting Invariants
- All repair escrow calculations must be deterministic and monotonic with respect to vehicle anomaly severity.
- Per-mile withholding rates must dynamically scale between baseline ($0.05/mi) and critical fault ($2.50+/mi) without ever producing negative ledger balances.
